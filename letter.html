A little letter, just for you

Dear Yuuji, my covey

Hi lovey! I'm not sure what happened today. I woke up feeling like you don't deserve someone like me. Ewan, I woke up with a full of thoughts about how I'm awful and bad for you. I hate it when this happens kasi I love you so much. I love you and you love me, I know that. You're the most special person to me.

When I get like this, I tend to over exert myself with doing things for you just to prove to myself that I am enough as it is to let myself stand by you. I overdo things just to convince myself na I'm worthy enough to walk beside you. I know naman na for you, I am enough, in fact I think na you think na I'm so much worthy of you. I just can't seem to convince myself. I'm sorry.

I'm really sorry na ganito ako today. I don't like feeling like this kasi I know na parang naiinvalidate ko yung feelings mo for me or that I'm doubting you or that I don't believe you. Believe me, I know naman what you feel. It's more on myself kasi. I feel like I'm not enough. I feel like ang daming mas better, women that are smarter, more talented, funnier, richer, more funny, those that are much much more beautiful than me. Someone na pag hinga palang niya makes her so worthy of you already. Anong laban ko sakanila, love.

But I'm not hear to push you away to other girls, my love. I'm here to say na despite my imperfections, despite me lacking everything good, my love for you has no limits. Whatever kind of love I am giving you, I m giving it all to you, without doubts, without restraints. I'm not really sure if the kind of love I give you is the love that you need, if it's the love that makes you feel peace and loved and good. I'm not sure if you feel what I feel where the love I feel for you feels so good, amazing, light, easy, and easy to breathe in.

I'm scared that I'm taking your life away from you. I'm scared na you're being forced to stay with me. I really scared of you feeling like nakakasakal ako. I hope na I make you feel as happy as you make me feel. I hope that you feel how much I love you. I promise that I will always love you, my love. That everyday, I love you more. I know na wala akong laban sa iba, pero all I know is that everyday, I promise you that I will and I am giving you all my love. That everyday, I try to figure out how to give you more love than the previous days.

My love for you can only grow more each day. I love you so much, Yuuji. I love you so much John. You are my love, my lovey, my covey, my future. You're the person I want to marry, and one day, I will.

With all my love,

Your person ♡
